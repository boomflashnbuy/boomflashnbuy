Store or Amazon: which is cheaper?

You are in front of the shelf, the price looks fine, but you wonder: what if it costs less online? Checking by hand means typing the right name and size and scrolling through results. With the barcode it takes a moment.

Compare the price in 4 steps

  1. Open BoomFlashnBuy on your phone (or say “Hey Siri, boom!” on iPhone).
  2. Point the camera at the barcode: at the beep the app recognises it.
  3. If you like, type the price you see in the store.
  4. Tap “Found! Go to prices”: Amazon opens with all the offers for that product.
The trick: the barcode (EAN/UPC) identifies the exact product, size included. So you compare the same thing, not something similar.

When the store wins

  • You need it now and don't want to wait for delivery.
  • The store has a deal or the same price as online.
  • You want to see it, try it or get advice.

When Amazon wins

  • The price is lower and you can wait for delivery.
  • You find sizes or multipacks that are better value.
  • You want to compare sellers and read reviews.

BoomFlashnBuy also keeps count: type the store price and what you paid on Amazon, and see how much you have saved in total.

FAQ

Do I need to install anything?

No. BoomFlashnBuy runs in your phone browser. If you like, add it to your Home Screen like an app.

Is it free?

Yes, free and with no sign-up. The app gets a small commission from Amazon on qualifying purchases, at no cost to you.

What if the product has no barcode?

Use “Read the label”: the app reads brand and model from the text. Or type or say what you are looking for.
