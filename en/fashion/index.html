Shoes and clothes: store or Amazon?

In fashion the same shoe or jacket can cost much less online, especially at the end of a season. Check before you queue at the till.

Where to find the code

  • Shoes: the barcode is on the label on the side of the box. Inside the tongue there is also the style code (e.g. Nike CW2288-111, Adidas B75806): the app reads it with “Read the label”.
  • Clothes and jackets: the barcode is on the price tag attached to the item.
  • Bags and accessories: price tag or inside label.

FAQ

Does it work with every brand?

Yes: if the product has a barcode or a style code, the app prepares the Amazon search for that exact product.

Is Amazon always cheaper?

No. Sometimes the store has a better deal or you need it now: the app just shows you the comparison, you decide.
