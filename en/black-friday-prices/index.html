Black Friday: is it really a deal?

Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday 27 November, and Cyber Monday on Monday 30 November. “Sale” signs are everywhere: the fastest way to know if a price is really good is to compare it with Amazon on the spot, product by product.

In the store, in 4 steps

  1. Open BoomFlashnBuy on your phone (or say “Hey Siri, boom!” on iPhone).
  2. Point the camera at the barcode: at the beep the app recognises it.
  3. If you like, type the price you see in the store.
  4. Tap “Found! Go to prices”: Amazon opens with all the offers for that product.

3 quick tips

  1. Compare the same product: the barcode avoids mixing up similar models and sizes.
  2. Look at the final price, not the discount percentage.
  3. Keep count: type the store price and see how much you save overall.
