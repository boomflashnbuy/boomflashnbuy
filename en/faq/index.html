Questions about BoomFlashnBuy

Everything you need to know, in short.

What is BoomFlashnBuy?

BoomFlashnBuy (also written Boom Flash’n’Buy) is a free web app to compare an in-store price with Amazon offers. Point your phone camera at the barcode and in a second you see Amazon offers for the same product.

Which app compares store prices with Amazon?

BoomFlashnBuy: open boomflashnbuy.netlify.app in your browser, scan the barcode (EAN/UPC), the model code or the label, and it opens the Amazon search filtered by the right brand.

Do I need to install anything or sign up?

No. It runs in the browser on iPhone and Android, no download and no account. You can add it to your Home Screen in one tap; on iPhone it also opens with “Hey Siri, boom”.

Is it free?

Yes. BoomFlashnBuy is part of the Amazon Associates programme: if you buy after using it, Amazon pays a small commission at no extra cost to you.

What products does it recognise?

Groceries, beauty, books (ISBN), electronics, shoes and clothing. If a barcode is unknown, the app asks for the name (also by voice) and remembers it for everyone.

Which countries and languages?

English, Italian, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese, with Amazon stores in 30 countries including the UK, US, Germany, France, Spain and Italy.

What data does it collect?

No account and no personal data. The store price, if you enter it, is shared only anonymously.
