Free barcode scanner

BoomFlashnBuy is a barcode scanner built for one thing: telling you in a flash whether the price in the store is a good one.

What it does

  • Reads EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC and other common formats, even tilted or far away.
  • Recognises the product and prepares the Amazon search in the right department.
  • No barcode? After 5 seconds it offers to read the label.
  • Works in 6 languages and uses your country's Amazon store.

How to use it

  1. Open BoomFlashnBuy on your phone (or say “Hey Siri, boom!” on iPhone).
  2. Point the camera at the barcode: at the beep the app recognises it.
  3. If you like, type the price you see in the store.
  4. Tap “Found! Go to prices”: Amazon opens with all the offers for that product.

Privacy

The camera is used only to read the code, inside the app. Searches and spending stay on your phone: no account needed.

FAQ

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. On iPhone open it in Safari (and with “Hey Siri, boom!”), on Android in Chrome, where you can also install it with one tap.

Why isn't the code being read?

Move closer, avoid reflections and turn on the torch with the 🔦 icon. If it still fails, use “Read the label”.
